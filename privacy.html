Privacy Policy

How I collect, use and protect your information when you visit this website or get in touch.

Effective date: November 12, 2026

This website, dominikas-kitchen.com, is run by Dominika Culliton, an independent Thermomix consultant based in Prosper, Texas ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information I collect, why, and the choices you have.

Information I collect

Information you give me. When you fill in the demo booking or "Join My Team" forms, I collect what you enter: your name, email address, phone number (optional), city or ZIP code, demo preferences, what you're interested in, and any message you write.

Information collected automatically. This site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use it, for example which pages are viewed, how long visits last, roughly where visitors are (city or country level), the device and browser used, and which buttons and links are clicked. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technology to do this.

Messages. If you contact me on WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook, those services handle your messages under their own privacy policies, and I see whatever you send me there.

How I use your information

  • To reply to you and arrange your demo, consultation or team chat
  • To follow up about your demo, your Thermomix purchase, or the consultant opportunity
  • To share recipes, tips and current offers if you've asked to hear from me
  • To understand how the website is used so I can improve it

I do not sell or rent your personal information, and I don't share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Services that process information for me

  • Web3Forms delivers form submissions from this site to my inbox.
  • Google Analytics (Google LLC) provides website statistics.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security.

Buying Thermomix or registering as a consultant

The "Buy" and "Register" buttons take you to the official Thermomix websites (shop.thermomix.com and register.thermomix.com), run by Vorwerk. Orders, payments, financing applications (Bread, Affirm) and consultant registrations are handled entirely on those sites under the Thermomix / Vorwerk privacy policy. I never see or store your payment details. As your consultant, Thermomix may share your order details and contact information with me so I can support you.

Cookies and your choices

  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site still works without them.
  • You can opt out of Google Analytics on all websites with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • You can ask me at any time to see, correct or delete the information you've sent me, or to stop contacting you.

How long I keep information

I keep form submissions and messages for as long as needed to help you and look after you as a customer or team member, and delete them when you ask me to or when they're no longer needed.

Security

This site uses HTTPS encryption. No method of sending or storing data online is completely secure, but I take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

This website is meant for adults. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
